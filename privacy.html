THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

What this website stores

FoodMania saves your cart and the delivery details you enter (such as your name, phone number, address, notes and selected payment method) in local storage in your browser so they can be available when you return to this website on the same browser and device. This information is not sent to a FoodMania website server by the current static-site code.

How WhatsApp orders work

When you choose to place an order, the website includes your order and delivery details in a WhatsApp link that opens in your browser. WhatsApp receives those details as part of opening the link; the order message is delivered to FoodMania when you press Send. WhatsApp is a separate service and its own privacy policy and terms apply to information it handles.

Chat assistant conversations

The FoodMania chat assistant saves its conversation in session storage so it can remain available while you move between pages in the same tab. This session data is normally cleared when that tab’s session ends. Do not enter passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive information into the chat assistant.

How to remove saved details

You can remove saved cart and delivery details by clearing this website’s local storage/site data in your browser settings. Closing the tab normally clears the chat assistant’s session storage. Clearing browser data may also remove other website preferences. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile may be able to view data saved there, so avoid saving sensitive information on a shared device.

Payments

This website does not collect card details or process online card payments. Payment arrangements are confirmed through the available checkout options and WhatsApp conversation.

Third-party services and hosting

The website loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts and Cloudflare’s CDN. When the site is hosted, the hosting provider may process technical request logs to operate and protect the service. Those third parties handle information under their own policies.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website’s features or data practices change. If you have a privacy question, contact FoodMania through WhatsApp.
