Good to know.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 35–45 min, depending on your area and how busy we are. We confirm the time on WhatsApp.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver to DHA, Clifton, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, PECHS, North Nazimabad. Not on the list? Message us and we'll check.

What does delivery cost? Is there a minimum order?

Delivery is Rs. 150, free over Rs. 2,000. The minimum order is Rs. 500.

How can I pay?

Pay cash on delivery, or by bank transfer, EasyPaisa or JazzCash. Choose at checkout. For transfers, we send the account details on WhatsApp.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you can. Once the kitchen has started your food, we can't cancel it.
